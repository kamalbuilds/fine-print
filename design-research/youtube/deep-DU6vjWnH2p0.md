## Techniques, with timestamps

- **1:32 Subtract.** "The question is not what you can add to make this more powerful and visual. It's what you can remove."
- **3:03 Effects compensate for weak layout.** Beginners rely on "Gradients, shadows, borders, 3D textures, fancy effects, because layout skills aren't solid yet."
- **3:34 The whole kit.** "One typography, two colors, one grid, images, and white space. That's it. Okay? Nothing else. No shadows, no texture, no patterns".
- **5:04 Gather real content first.** Pull the real text and assets into the file before laying anything out: "This is like grabbing all the ingredients".
- **5:34 Digest the content.** "it's not about just putting content and putting a width to the paragraph and just throwing it there, but rather sifting and understanding the content so that I can design for the content".
- **10:43 Promote a buried fact.** He pulls "expected building completion in 2027" out of the body copy and sets it as a big "by 2027".
- **11:15 Iterate layouts, not elements.** "There's 1,000 layouts that you can create with the same elements ... just duplicate it."
- **14:54 Spacing scale.** "usually it's 20 pixels, 40 pixels, 80 and 160", plus 10 for tight image groups; "12 column grid ... with 40 pixel margins".
- **16:57 Branching.** "I not only align elements vertically but I also ... align them horizontally", for example pushing the images down to line up with the nav.
- **18:30 Balance whitespace above and below a block.** Place a paragraph and CTA "as a whole thing as an organism": "40 pixels from the margin and then at 80 pixels from the nav".

## What applies to Fine Print

- The kit for Fine Print: one typeface family, two colours (ink plus one evidence tone), one grid, whitespace. The only "image" is the policy text itself. No shadows, no textures.
- Digest the content: the claim is the promoted fact. Set the six-word gist big, and keep the full quote at reading size beside it.
- Use a 10/20/40/80/160 spacing scale. Branch-align the gateway panel's top edge to the first diptych's top edge, and its rows to the diptych baselines.
- Iterate the diptych as a layout (claim left or right, the quote's measure, rule placement) using real policy text, never lorem ipsum.
