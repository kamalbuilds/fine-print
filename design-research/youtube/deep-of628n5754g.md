## Techniques, with timestamps

- **2:02 Five named failure modes.** "bland": the style you asked for "just isn't there". "too much": "crams text and numbers into every part of the screen". "wireframe": "strips everything down to a few gray boxes". "boxed": "your whole app gets shrunk down and stuck inside a card". "flat": "everything on the screen looks the same, so nothing really stands out".
- **3:02 Taste is decisions.** "most of taste is just small decisions you make over and over until you know which ones work. That's why he wrote his taste down."
- **4:34 Separate phone design.** "It treats the phone and the desktop as two different screens. While most agents just shrink the same design down".
- **5:04 One action, few beats.** "Every demo shows one action split into three to five small moments that play out in 10 to 16 seconds."
- **5:04 Copy a real example.** "rules on their own only got him so far. And the agent did a lot better when it had a real example to copy."
- **5:34 Few real words.** "It uses only three to seven real words on the whole screen and everything else becomes bars that stand in for text."
- **5:34 Motion needs a reason.** "every movement on screen needs a reason. The cursor moves in a curve like a real hand would, and each part slides into place instead of popping in."
- **6:05 Check the motion, not the still.** It "records the demo as it plays and lines up every moment of it side by side on one sheet", then checks for "text that doesn't fit its box or anything that jumps".
- **6:36 The check's limit.** "it can't tell you if the idea behind the demo is boring. So, you still have to watch the demo yourself."
- **9:39 Reduced motion.** Check "the still version people see when they've turned off animations".

## What applies to Fine Print

- Use the five failure modes as a review checklist for the result page. "Too much" is the main risk with six findings plus a gateway panel. "Boxed" is the risk if each diptych becomes a card.
- The verification moment (a quote checked against the page, then confirmed or struck) is the single action to stage. Give it three to five beats: quote appears, page lookup, match or strike.
- The strike-through should draw left to right, not pop in, and must have a reduced-motion static state.
- Verify motion with a frame sheet (capture several frames mid-animation), not one screenshot at rest.
- Design the phone diptych as a stacked claim-over-quote layout on purpose, rather than shrinking the two columns.
